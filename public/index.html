    04 / About

    Stuart Wilson

    Founder & Technical Director

    Stuart has spent 25 years building enterprise software. He started at Prudential in 1999 as a COBOL programmer, moved into Java, and became an application architect at Capita before contracting for Sky, National Records of Scotland, Registers of Scotland and HM Courts & Tribunals Service.

    He works best as a hands-on technical lead: writing the design, writing the code, and helping the team around him test it properly. He is comfortable in front of stakeholders, turning policy and business rules into software that does what it should.

    BSc (Hons) Mathematics & Business Studies, First Class · University of Edinburgh
